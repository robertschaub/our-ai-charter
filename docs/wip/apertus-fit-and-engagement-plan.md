# Apertus — fit & how to engage

*Working notes — rebuilt 2026-07-03 from primary-source verification; source-audit additions 2026-07-04 · WIP · public-safe. Companions: [Salathé actor note](public-ai-people-and-pathways.md), [channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md), [evaluation PoC scope](evaluation-poc-scope.md), [keystone memo](geneva-2027-keystone-memo.md), [artifacts roadmap](../Strategy/final-artifacts-roadmap.md), [initiation strategy](../Strategy/initiation-strategy.md), [actor & landscape map](../Evidence/actors-and-landscape.md), [verified findings](../Evidence/verified-findings.md).*

## In one line

The fit is real but **the evidence is one-sided**: the documented demand for an assurance/evaluation layer sits on the **Geneva 2027 summit side** — the prep process explicitly asks for "concrete tools beyond declarations" (evaluation benchmarks, trust labels) — **not** on the Apertus model-team side, which has voiced none publicly. So the initiative contributes the **governance-evidence/assurance layer**, treats Apertus as a **motivating open case (not a client)**, and engages the **ecosystem**, where **Public AI is an ally to align with, not a competitor**.

## 1. Grounding — what is verified (primary sources, 2026-07-03; selected facts refreshed 2026-07-28)

**Apertus (the model).** Fully open LLM (weights + data + recipe) from **EPFL, ETH Zurich and CSCS** under the **Swiss AI Initiative**; **Apache-2.0**; released **2 Sept 2025**; **8B / 70B** (plus a 2026 **Apertus Mini v1.1** 0.5/1.5/4B line; **Apertus 1.5** released 24 Jul 2026 — multimodal input, 262,144-token context, optional reasoning, with "Apertus 2" expected in months per the [deployer's release story](https://publicai.co/stories/apertus-1-5)); **~15T tokens**; **1,811 languages** per the 1.0 technical report (the project site now headlines "1000+"; the 1.5 report is announced as forthcoming). Distributed via **Swisscom** (strategic partner), **Hugging Face**, and the **Public AI** inference utility. Governance is **lean** — a Swiss AI Initiative **Steering Committee** (ETH + EPFL, equal voting power; **Antoine Bosselut** co-leads both the Initiative and Apertus — [swiss-ai.org](https://www.swiss-ai.org/team-3)) **+ dev team**, with **no publicly evident independent multistakeholder board or governance charter** (a fact to raise as a question, not a criticism). **Now in government production:** the canton of **Ticino** runs in-house document translation on a fine-tuned **Apertus-8B** (Artificialy-built; hosted in the cantonal data centre; ~100 staff; no data leaves Swiss infrastructure) — reported as the first production deployment of a nationally-built public-AI model in European government ([CSCS](https://www.cscs.ch/science/computer-science-hpc/2026/apertus-powers-in-house-ai-translation-for-ticino), [EPFL](https://actu.epfl.ch/news/apertus-powers-in-house-ai-translation-for-ticin-3/)). *My read: sensitive-document translation in real government use is precisely where independent assurance would matter.* Sources: [ETH release](https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2025/09/press-release-apertus-a-fully-open-transparent-multilingual-language-model.html), [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/swiss-ai/Apertus-70B-2509), [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-ai/fact-and-fiction-about-the-swiss-ai-model-apertus/90110034). *Corrections carried in from the earlier draft: the language count is the 1.0 technical report's **1,811** (site messaging says "1000+"), and the **CHF 20m is ETH-Domain backing for the initiative's next phase** (through 2028 per secondary reporting), not the original build grant.*

**Apertus's "Apertus Charter" (formerly "Swiss AI Charter") ≠ "Our AI Charter."** Apertus integrates an **Apertus Charter** — renamed from "Swiss AI Charter" with the 1.5 release ([release story](https://publicai.co/stories/apertus-1-5), 24 Jul 2026) — constitutional-values principles (neutrality, linguistic diversity, …) the model is *aligned to* via an LLM-as-judge scoring responses (technical-lead Imanol Schlag: *"nothing else specifically Swiss"* beyond it). That is a **model-alignment feature**, categorically different from **Our AI Charter** (a governance/assurance initiative); the residual name overlap is **nominal**, and the rename largely defuses it. Independent evidence that such alignment/transparency claims actually hold is exactly what an assurance layer would add. Sources: [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-ai/fact-and-fiction-about-the-swiss-ai-model-apertus/90110034), [Apertus tech report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14233).

**"Public AI" = three distinct layers — all allies, none the model-maker.**
- **Coalition** — [publicai.network](https://publicai.network/) (PAINT), Metagov-incubated; led in practice by Joshua Tan (product & strategy; whitepaper lead author Brandon Jackson's own site now leads with OpenFn — role drift noted 2026-07-28); partners incl. Open Future, Mozilla, Aspen Digital, Chatham House; chapters CH/US/UK. A movement, **not** an operating service.
- **Inference utility** — [publicai.co](https://publicai.co/), a self-described nonprofit chat+API launched 2 Sept 2025 serving Apertus + SEA-LION; runs on donated GPU + institutional compute across ~5 countries (CSCS, AWS, Exoscale, AI Singapore, Cudo, Jülich, Australia's NCI …). Its stack is itself open source ([`forpublicai`](https://github.com/forpublicai/): an OpenWebUI-based chat front end, a `litellm` model router, a Zuplo API gateway, and an MCP server).
- **Swiss cooperative** — **Public AI Switzerland / SPIU** ([publicai.ch](https://publicai.ch/)), a self-described customer-owned cooperative **in formation** (draft statutes naming «Genossenschaft Public AI», Liebefeld, dated **11 Aug 2026**; the notarised founding — required for cooperatives since 2023 — and register entry pending as of 2026-07-28), the Swiss chapter around the separately operated publicai.co utility; CHF 100 founding dues held in trust by Datalets.ch (Oleg Lavrovsky). Its usage claims are **self-reported and unaudited** (homepage counter 52,293 users on 2026-07-27, identical on 2026-07-28 — a static snapshot, not a live counter; scope unstated; the homepage headlines "#1 Apertus deployer worldwide", and the site repo's translation strings still carry "largest global deployer … over 45'000 registered users and trillions of tokens processed" — "trillions" conflicting with the 9.58B figure; repository copy, not found on the live pages checked, 2026-07-28); PublicAI likewise describes *itself* as Apertus's "official international deployer" at launch (a first-party figure of 115,000+ GPU-hours across 20 clusters). Operational deep-dive: [what they build](public-ai-what-they-build.md).
- A **Public AI Fellow, Switzerland** posting (Zürich; CHF 5,000 stipend; `josh@publicai.co`) was still listed as of 2026-07-27 with no public appointee announcement, remit including "Public AI's participation in the **Swiss AI Summit in February 2027**" (see §6). Sources: [publicai.network](https://publicai.network/), [publicai.co utility](https://publicai.co/stories/utility), [publicai.ch](https://publicai.ch/), [Fellow listing](https://publicai.network/jobs/fellow-switzerland).

**Geneva 2027 (the summit) and its stated demand.** The next intergovernmental AI summit (Bletchley → Seoul → Paris → New Delhi → **Geneva, 21–22 June 2027** → UAE 2028), announced by Federal President Parmelin at New Delhi on **19 Feb 2026**; run by **DETEC + FDFA** (project lead **Amb. Markus Reubi**; **Amb. Thomas Schneider**, OFCOM, on international relations). The April-2026 prep call (ICT4Peace / GenAI Zürich → *Plateforme Tripartite*) rewarded **"beyond declarations"** — concrete, reusable tools: governance toolkits, audit/transparency templates, **evaluation benchmarks, certification/trust labels**, and "tools to measure how AI may be skewed or manipulative before political decisions." Next *written-input* window **not yet dated** — but the open *Road to Geneva* pre-event registration opens ~Aug 2026 (see [channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md) §2b). Sources: [admin.ch](https://www.admin.ch/en/newnsb/qewY8BHWPhcMQEYV12fkr), [channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md).

## 2. Does the initiative add value? — yes, but the evidence is one-sided

- **To the ecosystem / summit: strong and documented.** The prep process is explicitly asking for the exact class of instrument this initiative builds — evaluation/assurance/trust-labels "beyond declarations" ([channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md)). This is the best-grounded value-add.
- **To Apertus the project: real but latent — and this is *our assessment*, not a request Apertus has made.** Apertus's transparency and Swiss-AI-Charter alignment are self- or tool-asserted; no one independently verifies that the claims hold. The GPO essay illustrates the common *openness = accountability* assumption that independent assurance would actually test.
- **Assessment, not fact: do not infer team demand from an external gap.** A documented gap ≠ the model team feeling urgency; open-model teams prioritise capability and adoption, and Apertus exposes **no public governance intake** ([channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md) §4). Route the demand through the channel that has actually voiced it — the summit — not the maintainers.

## 3. Good match? — complementary roles; everyone here is an ally

Apertus = the **open model** (makers: EPFL/ETH/CSCS). Public AI = **access / distribution** (coalition + utility + Swiss cooperative). This initiative = **governance + evidence + assurance + convening**. The roles are complementary; the natural counterparts are the **summit process, Public AI / Metagov, ICAIN, EPFL-AI-Center peers, and governance/assurance researchers** — not the Apertus engineers, for whom the layer is often simply irrelevant. Existing discipline holds: *Apertus as pilot anchor, not the whole frame; host / node / bridge, not owner; raise as a question, not criticism* ([initiation strategy](../Strategy/initiation-strategy.md)).

## 4. The two names — an alignment choice, not a collision

Both overlaps are with an ally or with a different kind of object, so neither is a threat:
- **"Our AI Charter" vs Apertus's "Apertus Charter"** (ex "Swiss AI Charter") — nominal only (a governance initiative vs a model-alignment feature; §1).
- **"Public AI Network" (this initiative's earlier building-block name) vs the PAINT coalition** — the coalition is an ally the initiative is *aligning with* (the [Fellow-Switzerland](https://publicai.network/jobs/fellow-switzerland) track — posting still listed 2026-07-27 — is a channel), but the shared name should be treated as a naming overlap until an explicit relationship exists.

The only real question is how to **signal the relationship cleanly**: **embrace** (keep "Public AI Network" as a contribution *into* the movement), **subordinate** (lead with functional names — "public-AI governance & evidence layer" — in Apertus-/summit-facing material), or **rename**. Recommendation: **subordinate for now**; move toward embrace only if the Public AI relationship becomes explicit.

## 5. How to engage — real channels only, results before asks

**Lead move:** an unprompted **evaluation proof-of-concept** ([scope](evaluation-poc-scope.md)) on an Apertus-*based* cited-source deployment — bring results, not a framework; name Apertus publicly only with partner agreement.

Grounded channels, in order of leverage:

1. **The Geneva 2027 prep process** — the channel that has *actually asked* for assurance/evaluation tools. The official *Road to Geneva* pre-event programme launches registration in **August 2026**; monitor the summit site, [dig.watch](https://dig.watch/processes/2027-geneva-ai-summit) and ICT4Peace for the next input window. The now-closed Swissnex "Geneva Loading…" fellowship (application deadline **12 Jul 2026**) remains evidence that one channel explicitly asked for an artifact ([channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md)).
2. **Public AI / Metagov, directly** — align, don't compete. Joshua Tan is the connective node; the [Fellow-Switzerland](https://publicai.network/jobs/fellow-switzerland) role (posting still listed 2026-07-27; no public appointee announcement) and the Swiss cooperative are concrete openings.
3. **ICAIN** (Katharina Frey) — an existing Swiss instance of internationally co-stewarded public AI.
4. **EPFL-AI-Center peers as *post-artifact* correction contacts, not first asks.** Per the [Salathé actor note](public-ai-people-and-pathways.md), Marcel Salathé (EPFL AI Center co-director; public Apertus interpreter — **not** a technical lead, **not** a Public AI / Metagov role-holder) is a plausible high-level *orientation / correction* bridge once a concrete artifact exists; for technical detail, Schlag / Bosselut / Jaggi or Swiss-AI-Initiative operational staff. Ask for correction on a bounded artifact, not endorsement.
5. **Apertus makers — lowest priority, and via the summit, not cold.** No public governance intake exists (Apache-2.0; de-facto `llm-requests@swiss-ai.org` or a GitHub issue); route an assurance/evaluation proposal through the summit process instead ([channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md)).

Keep the first ask **cheap to grant**.

**Near-term touchpoint — [Swiss {ai} Weeks 2026](https://ai-weeks.ch/) (1 Sep – 4 Oct).** The Apertus-anchored national event series (hackathons, talks, community events, mostly free) is a low-friction, no-gatekeeper way to meet the Swiss AI / Apertus community in person and, if useful, propose an evaluation/assurance session.
