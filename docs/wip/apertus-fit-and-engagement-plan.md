# Apertus — fit & how to engage

*Working notes — rebuilt 2026-07-03 from primary-source verification · WIP · public-safe. Companions: [Salathé actor note](public-ai-people-and-pathways.md), [channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md), [evaluation PoC scope](evaluation-poc-scope.md), [keystone memo](geneva-2027-keystone-memo.md), [artifacts roadmap](../Strategy/final-artifacts-roadmap.md), [initiation strategy](../Strategy/initiation-strategy.md), [actor & landscape map](../Evidence/actors-and-landscape.md), [verified findings](../Evidence/verified-findings.md).*

## In one line

The fit is real but **the evidence is one-sided**: the documented demand for an assurance/evaluation layer sits on the **Geneva 2027 summit side** — the prep process explicitly asks for "concrete tools beyond declarations" (evaluation benchmarks, trust labels) — **not** on the Apertus model-team side, which has voiced none publicly. So the initiative contributes the **governance-evidence/assurance layer**, treats Apertus as a **motivating open case (not a client)**, and engages the **ecosystem**, where **Public AI is an ally to align with, not a competitor**.

## 1. Grounding — what is verified (primary sources, 2026-07-03)

**Apertus (the model).** Fully open LLM (weights + data + recipe) from **EPFL, ETH Zurich and CSCS** under the **Swiss AI Initiative**; **Apache-2.0**; released **2 Sept 2025**; **8B / 70B** (plus a 2026 **Apertus Mini v1.1** 0.5/1.5/4B line); **~15T tokens**; **1,811 languages**. Distributed via **Swisscom** (strategic partner), **Hugging Face**, and the **Public AI** inference utility. Governance is **lean** — a Swiss AI Initiative **Steering Committee** (ETH + EPFL, equal voting power; **Antoine Bosselut** co-leads both the Initiative and Apertus — [swiss-ai.org](https://www.swiss-ai.org/team-3)) **+ dev team**, with **no publicly evident independent multistakeholder board or governance charter** (a fact to raise as a question, not a criticism). **Now in government production:** the canton of **Ticino** runs in-house document translation on a fine-tuned **Apertus-8B** (Artificialy-built; hosted in the cantonal data centre; ~100 staff; no data leaves Swiss infrastructure) — reported as the first production deployment of a nationally-built public-AI model in European government ([CSCS](https://www.cscs.ch/science/computer-science-hpc/2026/apertus-powers-in-house-ai-translation-for-ticino), [EPFL](https://actu.epfl.ch/news/apertus-powers-in-house-ai-translation-for-ticin-3/)). *My read: sensitive-document translation in real government use is precisely where independent assurance would matter.* Sources: [ETH release](https://ethz.ch/en/news-and-events/eth-news/news/2025/09/press-release-apertus-a-fully-open-transparent-multilingual-language-model.html), [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/swiss-ai/Apertus-70B-2509), [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-ai/fact-and-fiction-about-the-swiss-ai-model-apertus/90110034). *Corrections carried in from the earlier draft: it is **1,811 languages** (launch messaging said "1,000+"), and the **CHF 20m is future federal funding for the next training round**, not the original build grant.*

**Apertus's "Swiss AI Charter" ≠ "Our AI Charter."** Apertus integrates a **Swiss AI Charter** — constitutional-values principles (neutrality, linguistic diversity, …) the model is *aligned to* via an LLM-as-judge scoring responses (technical-lead Imanol Schlag: *"nothing else specifically Swiss"* beyond it). That is a **model-alignment feature**, categorically different from **Our AI Charter** (a governance/assurance initiative); the name overlap is **nominal**. Independent evidence that such alignment/transparency claims actually hold is exactly what an assurance layer would add. Sources: [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-ai/fact-and-fiction-about-the-swiss-ai-model-apertus/90110034), [Apertus tech report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14233).

**"Public AI" = three distinct layers — all allies, none the model-maker.**
- **Coalition** — [publicai.network](https://publicai.network/) (PAINT), Metagov-incubated; led by Joshua Tan + Brandon Jackson; partners incl. Open Future, Mozilla, Aspen Digital, Chatham House; chapters CH/US/UK. A movement, **not** an operating service.
- **Inference utility** — [publicai.co](https://publicai.co/), a self-described nonprofit chat+API launched 2 Sept 2025 serving Apertus + SEA-LION; runs on donated GPU + institutional compute across ~5 countries (CSCS, AWS, Exoscale, AI Singapore, Cudo, Jülich, Australia's NCI …). Its stack is itself open source ([`forpublicai`](https://github.com/forpublicai/): an OpenWebUI-based chat front end, a `litellm` model router, a Zuplo API gateway, and an MCP server).
- **Swiss cooperative** — **Public AI Switzerland / SPIU** ([publicai.ch](https://publicai.ch/)), a customer-owned cooperative **still being set up**, fronting the publicai.co utility; CHF 100 founding dues held in trust by Datalets.ch (Oleg Lavrovsky). Its "**#1 global deployer of Apertus**" / "45,000+ users" claims are **self-reported and unaudited** (publicai.co states 35,000+ for the same deployment); PublicAI likewise describes *itself* as Apertus's "official international deployer" at launch (a first-party figure of 115,000+ GPU-hours across 20 clusters).
- A live **Public AI Fellow, Switzerland** role is open (Zürich; CHF 5,000 stipend; `josh@publicai.co`), remit including "Public AI's participation in the **Swiss AI Summit in February 2027**" (see §6). Sources: [publicai.network](https://publicai.network/), [publicai.co utility](https://publicai.co/stories/utility), [publicai.ch](https://publicai.ch/), [Fellow listing](https://publicai.network/jobs/fellow-switzerland).

**Geneva 2027 (the summit) and its stated demand.** The next intergovernmental AI summit (Bletchley → Seoul → Paris → New Delhi → **Geneva, first half 2027** → UAE 2028), announced by Federal President Parmelin at New Delhi on **19 Feb 2026**; run by **DETEC + FDFA** (project lead **Amb. Markus Reubi**; **Amb. Thomas Schneider**, OFCOM, on international relations). The April-2026 prep call (ICT4Peace / GenAI Zürich → *Plateforme Tripartite*) rewarded **"beyond declarations"** — concrete, reusable tools: governance toolkits, audit/transparency templates, **evaluation benchmarks, certification/trust labels**, and "tools to measure how AI may be skewed or manipulative before political decisions." Next input window **not yet dated**. Sources: [admin.ch](https://www.admin.ch/en/newnsb/qewY8BHWPhcMQEYV12fkr), [channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md).

*Two framing corrections to the earlier draft: (1) the Geneva Policy Outlook essay ["The Swiss approach to AI sovereignty"](https://www.genevapolicyoutlook.ch/the-swiss-approach-to-ai-sovereignty/) (Dobos — Swisscom — & Singh, 26 Jan 2026) is **advocacy that does *not* name the "four gaps"** earlier attributed to it; it argues openness enables accountability and concludes optimistically. (2) Organizers have **not** been confirmed to designate Apertus "the flagship" or to name it in the official prep synthesis — it appears in surrounding commentary as an illustration. Verify the GPO wording in-browser before quoting (the page blocks automated fetch).*

## 2. Does the initiative add value? — yes, but the evidence is one-sided

- **To the ecosystem / summit: strong and documented.** The prep process is explicitly asking for the exact class of instrument this initiative builds — evaluation/assurance/trust-labels "beyond declarations" ([channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md)). This is the best-grounded value-add.
- **To Apertus the project: real but latent — and this is *our assessment*, not a request Apertus has made.** Apertus's transparency and Swiss-AI-Charter alignment are self- or tool-asserted; no one independently verifies that the claims hold. The GPO essay illustrates the common *openness = accountability* assumption that independent assurance would actually test.
- **Assessment, not fact: do not infer team demand from an external gap.** A documented gap ≠ the model team feeling urgency; open-model teams prioritise capability and adoption, and Apertus exposes **no public governance intake** ([channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md) §4). Route the demand through the channel that has actually voiced it — the summit — not the maintainers.

## 3. Good match? — complementary roles; everyone here is an ally

Apertus = the **open model** (makers: EPFL/ETH/CSCS). Public AI = **access / distribution** (coalition + utility + Swiss cooperative). This initiative = **governance + evidence + assurance + convening**. The roles are complementary; the natural counterparts are the **summit process, Public AI / Metagov, ICAIN, EPFL-AI-Center peers, and governance/assurance researchers** — not the Apertus engineers, for whom the layer is often simply irrelevant. Existing discipline holds: *Apertus as pilot anchor, not the whole frame; host / node / bridge, not owner; raise as a question, not criticism* ([initiation strategy](../Strategy/initiation-strategy.md)).

## 4. The two names — an alignment choice, not a collision

Both overlaps are with an ally or with a different kind of object, so neither is a threat:
- **"Our AI Charter" vs Apertus's "Swiss AI Charter"** — nominal only (a governance initiative vs a model-alignment feature; §1).
- **"Public AI Network" (this initiative's earlier building-block name) vs the PAINT coalition** — the coalition is an ally the initiative is *aligning with* (the open [Fellow-Switzerland](https://publicai.network/jobs/fellow-switzerland) track is a live channel), but the shared name should be treated as a naming overlap until an explicit relationship exists.

The only real question is how to **signal the relationship cleanly**: **embrace** (keep "Public AI Network" as a contribution *into* the movement), **subordinate** (lead with functional names — "public-AI governance & evidence layer" — in Apertus-/summit-facing material), or **rename**. Recommendation: **subordinate for now**; move toward embrace only if the Public AI relationship becomes explicit.

## 5. How to engage — real channels only, results before asks

**Lead move:** an unprompted **evaluation proof-of-concept** ([scope](evaluation-poc-scope.md)) on an Apertus-*based* cited-source deployment — bring results, not a framework; name Apertus publicly only with partner agreement.

Grounded channels, in order of leverage:

1. **The Geneva 2027 prep process** — the channel that has *actually asked* for assurance/evaluation tools. Monitor [dig.watch](https://dig.watch/processes/2027-geneva-ai-summit) / ICT4Peace for the next input window; the Swissnex "Geneva Loading…" fellowship (applications **12 Jul 2026**) is the one channel with a stated artifact ask ([channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md)).
2. **Public AI / Metagov, directly** — align, don't compete. Joshua Tan is the connective node; the open [Fellow-Switzerland](https://publicai.network/jobs/fellow-switzerland) role and the Swiss cooperative are concrete openings.
3. **ICAIN** (Katharina Frey) — an existing Swiss instance of internationally co-stewarded public AI.
4. **EPFL-AI-Center peers as *post-artifact* correction contacts, not first asks.** Per the [Salathé actor note](public-ai-people-and-pathways.md), Marcel Salathé (EPFL AI Center co-director; public Apertus interpreter — **not** a technical lead, **not** a Public AI / Metagov role-holder) is a plausible high-level *orientation / correction* bridge once a concrete artifact exists; for technical detail, Schlag / Bosselut / Jaggi or Swiss-AI-Initiative operational staff. Ask for correction on a bounded artifact, not endorsement.
5. **Apertus makers — lowest priority, and via the summit, not cold.** No public governance intake exists (Apache-2.0; de-facto `llm-requests@swiss-ai.org` or a GitHub issue); route an assurance/evaluation proposal through the summit process instead ([channel demand](../Evidence/geneva-2027-channel-demand.md)).

Keep the first ask **cheap to grant**.
