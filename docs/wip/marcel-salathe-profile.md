## Bottom line

Marcel Salathé is a high-leverage **Swiss AI ecosystem bridge**, not a verified PublicAI / publicai.ch / Metagov governance actor. His public relevance for Our AI Charter is strongest through four channels:

1. **EPFL AI Center / SNAI / Apertus orbit** -- institutional proximity to the Swiss open-model stack that this initiative uses as its first motivating case.
2. **Public argument for open, reproducible Swiss AI** -- his own public writing frames Apertus as a smart sovereignty investment because it builds capability, transparency, and local expertise without pretending to deliver full sovereignty.
3. **Public-facing convening** -- Inside AI, AMLD, Swiss {ai} Weeks, and the EPFL AI Center's civic and International Geneva programs put him near the audiences this initiative must understand.
4. **Citizen and science-policy legitimacy** -- his EPFL citizens' assembly and CH++ work overlap with the Charter's accountability, democratic control, and evidence-to-policy logic.

The practical reading: he is a **useful Apertus/SNAI-adjacent orientation or correction contact once there is a concrete artifact**, not the first place to ask for adoption. Keep the Public AI / Metagov route separate unless a direct relationship is verified.

## Strategic reading

### Why he matters

Salathé sits at a rare intersection for this repository:

- **Technical public model credibility:** close to EPFL AI Center, SNAI and the public story of Apertus, while not being the named technical lead.
- **Public sovereignty argument:** he has already made the "sovereignty is a spectrum" case around Apertus. That is close to this repository's "network, not fortress" framing.
- **Trust and reproducibility vocabulary:** his Apertus writing emphasizes open training pipelines, transparent data, compliance, and reproducibility. The Charter adds the next layer: independent evidence, redress, allocation/accountability rules, and anti-capture governance for deployments.
- **Democratic legitimacy:** the citizens' assembly gives him a public, source-backed connection to citizen participation and human control, not only model-building.
- **Convening reach:** Inside AI, AMLD, Swiss {ai} Weeks, Geneva AI Initiative, and CH++ make him an ecosystem interpreter across research, policy, public adoption, and civic competence.

### What not to infer

- No public evidence in this pass that Salathé holds a role in **PublicAI**, **publicai.ch / SPIU**, **Metagov**, or **Current AI**.
- No public evidence in this pass that he controls Apertus governance, SNAI decision rights, or the official Geneva 2027 process.
- Public posts and podcasts show interest and influence; they are not partnership, endorsement, or availability.
- The EPFL AI Center bridge should not be collapsed into the whole Apertus team. The named Apertus technical leads in the repo remain Imanol Schlag, Antoine Bosselut, and Martin Jaggi.
