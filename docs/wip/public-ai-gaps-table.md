# Public AI — observed gaps, and what would close them

*Offered as ally input to the Public AI Inference Utility and Public AI Switzerland. Context: we reviewed what the Public AI family builds and operates, from its own published sources — site, configs, tracker, statutes, terms ([full deep-dive](public-ai-what-they-build.md)). The utility is real, useful, and unusually transparent in its code. The ten gaps below are where its **operational claims cannot yet be checked** — two are items Public AI has already named itself (marked **self-identified**), so part of this table documents demand for work already scoped. "What would close it" is deliberately provider-neutral: every closure stands on its own, whoever supplies it.*

## Why closing these is becoming commercially load-bearing

Beyond the accountability case, the evidence these gaps ask for is starting to be priced. Aon publishes that underwriters "increasingly expect" clear evidence of AI governance and documented model testing, and that organisations able to demonstrate it "will secure cleaner terms, greater capacity and more favorable outcomes" ([*AI Risk 2026*](https://www.aon.com/en/insights/articles/ai-risk-2026-practical-agenda), 7 May 2026) — while the Lloyd's Market Association reports its members currently cannot see insureds' AI use at all, so "little data on AI usage is currently collected during underwriting" ([AI loss scenarios survey](https://lmalloyds.com/campaigns/understanding-ai-exposures-ai-loss-scenarios-survey-results/), surveyed mid-2025). And from **9 December 2026**, under the EU Product Liability Directive ([2024/2853](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32024L2853)), software is a product and a defendant's failure to disclose relevant evidence creates a rebuttable presumption of defectiveness (Art. 10(2)(a)). Serving attestation (gap 1), an incident record (gap 4), and a per-object data-flow statement (gap 9) are the same artifacts an enterprise customer's insurer and procurement reviewer will ask to see. Full sourcing and grading: [AI liability and insurance evidence](ai-liability-and-insurance-evidence.md).
