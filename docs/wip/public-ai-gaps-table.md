# Public AI — observed gaps, and what would close them

*Offered as ally input to the Public AI Inference Utility and Public AI Switzerland. Context: we reviewed what the Public AI family builds and operates, from its own published sources — site, configs, tracker, terms ([full deep-dive](public-ai-what-they-build.md)). The utility is real, useful, and unusually transparent in its code. The gaps below are where its **operational claims cannot yet be checked** — most of them are items Public AI has already named itself (marked **self-identified**), so this table mostly documents demand for work already scoped. "What would close it" is deliberately provider-neutral: every closure stands on its own, whoever supplies it.*

## Operational / technical

| # | Gap | Evidence (public, dated) | Status | What would close it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Serving provenance** — a user cannot verify which backend, operator, or jurisdiction served a request; the `Inference-Id` response header copies the upstream completion ID only | [Header module](https://github.com/forpublicai/platform.publicai.co/blob/main/modules/add-inference-id-header.ts); [issue #35](https://github.com/forpublicai/chat.publicai.co/issues/35) (open since Oct 2025, maintainers engaged, third-party verification PoC referenced); [infra-engineer posting](https://metagov.org/join/jobs/public-ai-founding-infrastructure-engineer): users "should be able to see — and verify — which backend served their inference" (all checked 2026-07-27/28) | **Self-identified** | Signed per-response serving attestation (backend, region, model, timestamp); integrate the verification PoC already welcomed in the thread |
| 2 | **Chat "served by" attribution is not runtime data** — the published function derives the displayed sponsor from the *requested model*, with a weighted random choice among sponsors for Apertus | [sponsor_attribution.py](https://github.com/forpublicai/chat.publicai.co/blob/main/community/owui_functions/sponsor_attribution.py) (2026-07-27; whether this exact function is deployed is not determinable from source) | Observed (published code) | Drive the attribution from the router's actual lane metadata — the same fix as #1, surfaced in the UI |
| 3 | **Guardrails: description vs published config** — the June 2026 architecture describes Bedrock Guardrails, but the published gateway config attaches the policy only to a deprecated internal route; the active `/v1/chat/completions` carries none | [AWS APN write-up](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/apn/how-public-ai-delivers-sovereign-llm-inference-on-aws-and-intel/) vs [routes.oas.json](https://github.com/forpublicai/platform.publicai.co/blob/main/config/routes.oas.json) (2026-07-28; live state unverifiable externally) | Observed (config conflict) | Attach the policy to the active routes, or publish the actual moderation posture so description and deployment match |
| 4 | **TLS verification disabled globally** in the published router settings (`ssl_verify: false` in `litellm_settings`, plus per-model flags) | [LiteLLM ConfigMap](https://github.com/forpublicai/chat.publicai.co/blob/main/charts/web_services/charts/litellm/templates/configmap.yaml) (2026-07-28; deployed scope unverified) | Observed (config) | Enable verification; where a partner lane needs an exception, pin its CA and document the exception |
| 5 | **No status page, SLA, uptime or incident history** — the terms disclaim availability entirely | status.publicai.co does not resolve (2026-07-27); [terms](https://publicai.co/tc); posting lists "downtime warnings" as work to build | **Self-identified** | A public status page + incident log — even best-effort, it is the minimum utility furniture the public-infrastructure framing implies |
| 6 | **Silent cross-border and cross-model fallbacks** — the router config fails over across countries *and* models (e.g. Apertus-70B → Qwen-SEA-LION-32B) without telling the user | [Model YAMLs](https://github.com/forpublicai/chat.publicai.co) (2026-07-27) | Observed (config) | Disclose fallback in the response (header/UI); offer a strict-jurisdiction / strict-model mode that fails rather than substitutes |

## Accountability / claims

| # | Gap | Evidence (public, dated) | Status | What would close it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Usage counters are self-reported with scope unstated** — 52,293 users · 2,166 developers · 9.58B tokens on publicai.ch; whether Swiss-only or utility-wide is not said | [publicai.ch](https://publicai.ch/) (2026-07-27); no third-party confirmation found | Observed (claims) | Publish counter definitions and scope (what counts as a user; which deployment); a one-paragraph methodology note would do |
| 8 | **Partner claims are asymmetric** — partnerships shown on publicai.ch (ETH AI Center, EPFL AI Center, Swiss {ai} Weeks) are not mirrored on the partner pages checked | [ai-weeks.ch](https://ai-weeks.ch/) and ETH's Apertus release (2026-07-27) | Observed (claims) | Link a partner-side confirmation per logo, or reword to the actual relationship level |
| 9 | **Stale superlative copy contradicts live figures** — "#1 global deployer", "35,000+ users", "trillions of tokens" survive in repo/README copy against the live 9.58B counter | [Site repo README](https://github.com/forpublicai/publicai.ch) vs live counters (2026-07-27) | Observed (claims) | One canonical figure set; sweep the old copy |
| 10 | **No accountability mechanism over the utility itself yet** — the dialogue, benchmarks, and cooperative governance sit *beside* the service; nothing independently attests its operational claims | Deep-dive [§7 reading](public-ai-what-they-build.md), from the sources above | Observed (structural) | Name the oversight venue (cooperative assembly? advisory group?) and invite independent review of a small, defined claim set — provenance (#1) is the natural first candidate |

## Data residency / privacy

| # | Gap | Evidence (public, dated) | Status | What would close it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | **"Hosted in Switzerland" vs the global terms** — publicai.ch's draft privacy says services and data are hosted in Switzerland; the utility's terms describe a US-based nonprofit, prompts "sent directly to an AI developer", and subprocessors across jurisdictions | [publicai.ch/privacy](https://publicai.ch/privacy.html) vs [publicai.co/tc](https://publicai.co/tc) (2026-07-27) | Observed (docs conflict) | A per-object data-flow statement (accounts · chat history · prompt processing · logs) with locations; scope the Swiss claim to the objects it is true for |
| 12 | **Model-provider retention unspecified** — the terms name external model providers but not what those providers retain | [publicai.co/tc](https://publicai.co/tc) (2026-07-27) | Observed | State per-provider retention, or link each provider's terms from the model catalog |
